## CATATAN PENOMORAN INVOICE

| Kode | Keterangan |
|------|-----------|
| `HB-INV-[TAHUN]-[NNN]` | Invoice proyek (termin 1-4) |
| `HB-MNT-[TAHUN]-[BLN]-[NNN]` | Invoice maintenance bulanan |
| `HB-CR-[TAHUN]-[NNN]` | Invoice change request / pekerjaan tambahan |

**Contoh:** `HB-INV-2025-001`, `HB-MNT-2025-07-001`
